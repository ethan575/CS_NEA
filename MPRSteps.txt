⭐ The algorithm in 3 phases
This is the version used in ODE and many indie engines.

⭐ Phase 1 — Find the “portal”
You start by finding a direction from A to B:

Code
d = centerB - centerA
Then compute:

Code
v0 = support(d)
v1 = support(perpendicular to d)
v2 = support(perpendicular to both)
These three points form a triangle on the Minkowski difference.

This triangle is your portal.

⭐ Phase 2 — Portal refinement
Now you repeatedly:

Compute the normal of the portal triangle

Shoot a support point in that direction

If the new point is “in front” of the portal → replace one vertex

If the new point is “behind” → you’ve found the closest face

This converges extremely fast.

Unlike EPA, you never build a polytope.
You only ever maintain one triangle.

⭐ Phase 3 — Extract contact info
Once the portal stops changing:

The portal normal is the collision normal

The distance from origin to the portal plane is the penetration depth

The barycentric coordinates of the origin projected onto the triangle give the contact point (no clue what that is so just ignore it for the sec)

Done.
